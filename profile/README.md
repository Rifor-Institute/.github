# RIFOR — Resonance Institute for Open Research

A research home for independent minds.

RIFOR provides research membership, public researcher profiles, and privacy-respecting infrastructure for researchers working outside conventional institutions.

## What we support

- Human-reviewed membership applications.
- A stable researcher identity and hosted public profile, with confidential identity options.
- Member email accounts and practical guidance for maintaining a researcher email and profile.
- Clear, honest guidance on affiliation, manuscript submission, and independent research.

## Clear boundaries

RIFOR membership is not a university appointment, an academic degree, a fellowship, or peer-review certification. It does not endorse a member’s research findings or guarantee publication or acceptance by external platforms. Members retain ownership and authorship of their work.

See our [membership agreement](https://rifor.org/legal/membership-agreement.pdf) and [FAQ](https://rifor.org/faq) for the scope and terms of membership.
